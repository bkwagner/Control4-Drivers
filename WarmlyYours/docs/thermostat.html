WarmlyYours Thermostat (OJ Cloud)

One WarmlyYours Wi-Fi floor-heating thermostat, shown in Control4 as a standard thermostat. This driver doesn't connect to the cloud itself. It binds to the WarmlyYours (OJ Cloud) account driver, which passes it the thermostat's live state and sends its changes back.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by WarmlyYours, OJ Electronics or Control4.

Setup

  1. Set up the WarmlyYours (OJ Cloud) account driver first and confirm it has signed in.
  2. Add this driver to the thermostat's room.
  3. On the Connections tab, bind its WarmlyYours Thermostat connection to the matching thermostat on the account driver.
  4. Serial ID fills in and Driver Status shows Online once it has received the thermostat.

What it shows and controls

  • Temperature: the floor temperature, which the thermostat reports about once a minute.
  • Heat setpoint: the target floor temperature, in 1 °F or 0.5 °C steps.
  • Heating: whether the floor is heating right now.
  • Scale: follows the project's °F/°C setting and can be changed in Navigator.

Modes and holds

In Control4What the thermostat does
Mode HeatRuns its own weekly schedule.
Mode OffHolds permanently at the thermostat's minimum (41 °F / 5 °C), which protects against frost. Set the mode back to Heat to resume the schedule.
Hold OffFollows the schedule.
Hold Until NextHolds the current setpoint until the next schedule event, then resumes the schedule. The app calls this Comfort mode.
Hold PermanentHolds the setpoint until changed. The app calls this Manual mode.

Changing the setpoint while following the schedule starts an Until Next hold, like changing it on the thermostat or in the app. During a Permanent hold it changes the held temperature instead.

Schedule

The thermostat's weekly schedule appears in the standard Control4 thermostat scheduling screen, with six events per day. Enabled events are the ones active on the thermostat.

  • Editing an event in Control4 changes the schedule on the thermostat itself, and the app shows the change.
  • Avoid editing the schedule in the app and in Control4 at the same time.
  • Some thermostats share one program across several days, for example all weekdays. Editing any of those days changes all of them, as it does on the thermostat.
